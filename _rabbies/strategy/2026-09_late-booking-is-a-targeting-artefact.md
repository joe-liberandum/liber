# Late booking is an artefact of late targeting

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Argument delivered August 2026 in the combined time-in-market deck, v4.

## Recommendation

Hold the position that Rabbie's booking data reflects a late buying strategy rather than a late deciding customer, and use it to argue for spend moved into an earlier window. Do not present the size of the recoverable window as known.

## Why

Booker interviews consistently report consideration windows of months. Every one of those bookings appears in GA4 as a first-session booking. GA4 is accurate but pointed at the final act of booking, so it produces a targeting bias, not a measurement error. The Safari seven-day cookie cap and cross-device identity loss both compress observed windows in the same direction.

Australia functions as a natural experiment: different spend timing, earlier booking pattern.

The economics of the current window are deteriorating on Rabbie's own August figures. Google return on ad spend on non-brand campaigns fell from 9.8 to 4.3 between January and July. Meta cost per booking rose from £17 to £49 between May and July against the same months last year. First visits rose from 51% to 77%. More new people arriving, fewer of them buying, at rising cost. In the final three weeks the customer has already decided to take a tour, so everyone bids on the same words for the same person on the same day.

The framing line worth keeping: we are not found late because people decide late, we are found late because late is the only place we look.

## What this rests on

- A2: late booking is an artefact of late targeting, not late deciding. Mechanism well argued, size of the recoverable window not established.
- A3: the 92% interest and desire drop-off is addressable rather than category-normal. Never benchmarked. If high drop-off is normal for a considered, date-dependent purchase, this argument reframes.

## What would change this

Spend moved into an earlier window producing no incremental bookings over a full season. Or attribution work showing the long consideration windows customers report do not correspond to trackable earlier site visits, which would mean the demand exists but is not reachable earlier.

## Options considered and rejected

**Presenting booking data as evidence of customer behaviour.** Rejected. Booking data cannot validate the strategy that produced it. This is the core of the argument.

**Recommending a full spend reallocation.** Rejected. The mechanism justifies a test, not a reallocation. Deposits give early marketing something to say and provide the cleanest before-and-after available.

## Next

Deposits are the enabling piece and are time-critical. Record where multi-day booking sits today before launch: how far ahead people book, how many reach basket, how many pay. Once deposits are live the before-state cannot be recovered and the value of the change will never be known.
