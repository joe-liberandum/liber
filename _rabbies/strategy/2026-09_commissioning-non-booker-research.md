# Commissioning non-booker and abandoner research

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Options paper with a clear preference. Needs a commercial conversation, not more planning.

## Recommendation

Commission abandoner and non-booker research as a separate piece of work, priced as an additional service rather than absorbed into Package B hours. It is the single commission that would most change the confidence ratings across the whole engagement.

## Why

Every persona is built from people who chose Rabbie's, or people shown the site by a moderator. Non-bookers, cart abandoners and competitor-only bookers have not been interviewed. Nothing in the current evidence base can describe why people leave. It describes why people who stayed, stayed.

That boundary is currently carried as a caveat on slides. It is also the binding constraint on nine initiatives:

- Deposits, confidence 0.55, with no abandoners interviewed and no persona assigned
- Late availability, persona marked "To discover", reach scored 2 out of 5 for that reason alone, on a page about to receive top-of-navigation traffic
- Buy Box, two sub-tasks at 0.5 with personas inferred
- Persist session, 0.5, one annoyance report
- Invite to tour, 0.3, inferred from a single participant
- Private tours, 0.6, new segment, only two private-tour buyers ever interviewed
- Plus PLP filters, customer support, and the mobile PDP traffic-quality question

Money is being committed against these estimates now. Deposits is being scoped and Buy Box design is under way.

## What this rests on

- A1: the personas describe the market. This memo exists because that assumption is the largest exposure in the engagement and cannot be closed from inside the current sample.
- A3: the 92% drop-off is addressable. Abandoner research is also the only route to testing that, and it has never been benchmarked.
- A8: deposits recover hesitant bookers. Confidence low, and unresolvable without this work.

## What would change this

Abandoner research being genuinely unrecruitable. Exit-survey recruitment has methodology constraints and non-converting private tour enquirers are unreachable through booker-only channels. If recruitment fails, the answer is a smaller-scope substitute, not carrying the gap silently.

## Options considered and rejected

**Absorbing it into Package B research hours.** Rejected. Package B is 24 hours a month covering quarterly sprints, monthly voice-of-customer synthesis, usability tracking and one feature validation. Adding a new-methodology study displaces committed work.

**Waiting until the current initiatives ship and reading the results.** Rejected. Post-hoc reads cannot distinguish a wrong hypothesis from a poor execution, and by then the money is spent.

**Doing it cheaply through an exit survey alone.** Partly viable and worth scoping as the fallback, but a survey establishes stated reasons, not behaviour, and the whole point is that stated reasons from bookers are already what the engagement has.

## Next

Two things, both yours. Price the commission. Then raise it at a monthly strategic review with the deposit and late availability examples attached, because those two make the cost of not knowing concrete rather than abstract.

Also worth pairing: two or three UK last-minute booker interviews in an April to June window would de-risk late availability specifically, at much smaller scope.
