# Four personas as the shared frame across three agencies

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Decision taken April 2026 on delivery of the persona and navigation work.

## Recommendation

Keep the four personas as the single customer frame across Sunny Lemons, Liberandum and Salience. Do not build a fifth. Sequence work by persona confidence: build against the Meticulous Planner and Pragmatic Day-Tripper now, treat Purposeful Adventurer and Trusted Returner work as hypothesis generating.

## Why

Three agencies working the same site with three customer models produces incoherent output and endless relitigating. One frame, adopted by all three, is worth more than a marginally better frame held by one.

The confidence ratings are the load-bearing part and they are what stops this becoming dogma. Meticulous Planner 90%, Pragmatic Day-Tripper 78%, Purposeful Adventurer 70%, Trusted Returner 65%. Segment D strengthened the top two without contradicting the set. Nothing since has produced a motivation the four do not cover.

Private tours is the test case that proved the model flexible. The private-tour pull turned out to be a need state layered on the existing four rather than a fifth persona: the people drawn to it diverge on discovery, decision pattern and value exchange, sharing only the private-tour interest. Handled as a page workstream, not a persona.

## What this rests on

- A1: the personas describe the market, not just the people who bought. Every interview to date is with a booker or a moderated new visitor. This is the single largest exposure in the engagement.

## What would change this

Abandoner or cart-abandoner research surfacing a dominant motivation none of the four hold. Or an OTA-only booker segment whose reasoning contradicts the model.

Two segments are already logged with no persona behind them: late availability bookers, marked "To discover", and group and private hire. Either could break the set. Reach and impact scores for initiatives serving them are currently guesses.

## Options considered and rejected

**A fifth private-tours persona.** Rejected. Only two private-tour buyers have been interviewed and the signals are latent demand and page defects, not a researched buyer journey.

**Separate persona sets per agency.** Rejected. The coordination cost exceeds any gain in fit.

**Waiting for abandoner research before adopting.** Rejected at the time and still right. The work could not wait, and the confidence ratings carry the caveat honestly.
