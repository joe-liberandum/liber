# Owned attributed reviews replace the Trustpilot feed as the trust surface

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Recommendation made in Segment D research, June 2026. Not yet built.

## Recommendation

Build a catalogue of owned, verified, tour-attributed reviews and make it the primary trust surface on the PDP. Separate the problem claim from the solution claim in every deck: high confidence that the current section breaks trust, medium confidence that owned reviews are the fix.

## Why

The reviews section actively breaks trust rather than failing to build it. Two Segment D participants independently lost confidence there, and one moved from interested to a firm no almost entirely on that basis. The feed is thin and unattributed. Photos are not tagged to tours, so a customer cannot tell whether a review photo shows the tour they are looking at.

Rated 95% confidence in Segment D research, the highest on the backlog, and the single clearest conversion blocker that research identified.

The model as defined: tokenised magic link, guide-prompted QR scan at the peak emotional moment during the tour, mandatory publication of negative reviews, referral prompt at confirmation. Mandatory negative publication is the part that makes the rest credible and is the part most likely to get negotiated away. Hold it.

The guide connection matters. Guides drive rebooking and reviews mentioning guides are the raw material for the PDP guide module, which is currently blocked on content that operations has not been asked for. One collection mechanism serves both.

## What this rests on

- A7: owned attributed reviews will fix the trust break. High on the problem, medium on the solution. The evidence establishes the current section breaks trust; it does not establish that ownership rather than volume, recency or photo attribution is what fixes it.
- A1: the personas describe the market. The two participants who lost trust were moderated new visitors, which is the right population for this finding, so this is one of the better-evidenced items.

## What would change this

Owned reviews shipping at volume with no movement in PDP-to-booking conversion. That would point at volume or recency as the operative factor rather than ownership.

Photo attribution shipping alone and closing most of the gap, which would make the full owned-review programme unnecessary.

## Options considered and rejected

**Increasing Trustpilot volume.** Rejected. It does not solve attribution to specific tours or guides, and Trustpilot is the wrong proof for private tours regardless.

**Photo attribution alone.** Not rejected, deferred. It is a real option and cheaper in principle, but it is a bigger job than it looks: backend tagging, review system changes, display work. Worth scoping as a standalone before committing to the full programme.

**Promoting reviews above the fold on mobile first.** Rejected for now. Promoting a trust-breaking module up the page could make things worse. See the mobile PDP test note.
