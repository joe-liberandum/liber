# Qualification work moves from the booking flow to the PDP

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Decision taken during Direct Packages 1.0 in 2025, carried into 2.0 as the core argument.

## Recommendation

Move eligibility, accommodation, mobility and expectation-setting content out of the booking flow and onto the PDP, as a standing principle rather than a one-off fix. Judge any new booking-flow requirement against it: if a customer needs the information to decide, it belongs before the flow.

## Why

The multi-day flow loses 60% at the accommodation step and 60% at personal details. Three participants entered checkout specifically to find accommodation information, meaning research-intent users are pulled into the funnel before they are ready and inflate false-entry abandonment.

The information mostly exists. It is in a guide rather than on the tour page. One customer did not know Rabbie's booked the hotels until she was told in an interview. Another assumed it without the page saying so. That is a placement problem, not a content problem, and placement is cheap.

The same logic covers mobility. A participant planning for an 85-year-old traveller spent a sustained part of her session failing to find mobility information and concluded she would have to phone. Support hours do not cover North America. So the fallback channel for a qualification question is closed during her waking hours.

Late-stage surprises cause abandonment. This principle is the cheapest structural improvement available on the site.

## What this rests on

- A5: moving qualification upstream reduces booking-flow abandonment. Medium to high. The behaviour is established; that the 60% are those people is not.
- A3: the drop-off is addressable rather than category-normal.

## What would change this

User testing showing the accommodation-step drop is driven by price or genuine disqualification rather than missing information. Or PDP accommodation surfacing shipping with the booking-flow drop unchanged.

If the drop is price, the work is merchandising and belongs to a different team.

## Options considered and rejected

**Improving the accommodation step in place.** Rejected. It keeps research-intent traffic inside the funnel and treats the symptom.

**Gating the flow behind a qualification question.** Rejected. Adds a step for people who are ready, and Rabbie's tendency is already to over-engineer responses to small feedback volumes.

**Waiting for the user test before moving anything.** Partly rejected. The accommodation content move is justified on placement grounds alone. The step redesign is not, and should wait.

## Next

Direct Packages 2.0 requires user testing before design to confirm root cause on both drops. That sequencing has been overtaken by the build. Record what was and was not tested before build so the post-launch read is honest about what it can attribute.
