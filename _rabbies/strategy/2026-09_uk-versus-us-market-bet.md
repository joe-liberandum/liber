# UK growth versus US recovery

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Options paper. The bet is already being made implicitly through prioritisation.

## Recommendation

Continue backing UK-facing work, because it is cheap and the copy is wrong regardless of which market grows. But stop making deferral decisions on the strength of US decline until someone establishes why UK is up and whether US decline is cyclical or structural.

The distinction matters: local content at 2.5 days is worth doing either way. Deferring 25 days of account work partly because it serves the US is a different kind of decision and rests on a belief nobody has tested.

## Why

UK is now 72% of US demand, up from 54%. US non-brand demand is down 40% year on year. Those two figures are driving decisions across the backlog.

The UK copy problem stands on its own merits. UK customers already know Scotland, care more about price, book later, and trust different signals, ABTA and ATOL rather than the current set. The site addresses them as first-time visitors to the country. That is wrong today at 72% of US demand and would still be wrong at 40%. 2.5 days, RICE rank 2.

The deferrals are where the bet gets expensive. Plan-multiple-tours was pushed to Q4 partly because it mostly serves US customers. Loyalty recognition was pushed partly because it serves the declining market. My New Account went with them. That is roughly 25 days plus of work deferred on a market read that has never been examined.

Nobody has established why UK is up. Without that, backing UK is partly blind, and the ratio could be moving because the denominator is falling rather than because the numerator is rising. A 40% US fall would move that ratio on its own with no UK growth at all.

## What this rests on

- A12: UK growth is the better bet than US recovery. Medium confidence.
- A1: the personas describe the market. The booker sample skews US and multi-day, so the persona evidence describes the market Rabbie's is moving away from better than the one it is moving toward.

## What would change this

US non-brand demand recovering on its own, indicating a cyclical dip rather than a structural shift. Three deferrals would then be wrong.

Decomposition showing UK absolute volume is flat and the ratio moved because US fell. That would reframe UK from a growth market to a survivor, and the case for building for it would weaken considerably.

## Options considered and rejected

**Rebalancing the whole roadmap toward UK.** Rejected. The evidence supports a copy and trust-signal change, not a strategic reallocation.

**Treating US decline as recoverable and continuing to build for it.** Rejected on cost. Rising acquisition cost in the US window is documented: Google return on ad spend fell from 9.8 to 4.3, Meta cost per booking rose from £17 to £49.

**Waiting for market clarity before acting.** Rejected for the copy work, which is cheap and right either way. Accepted for anything larger.

## Next

Ask for UK and US absolute volumes rather than the ratio, and a read on whether the US fall is category-wide or Rabbie's specific. Cheap, and it decides whether three deferrals were right.
