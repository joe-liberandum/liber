# Five initiatives deferred, and why

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Supersedes an earlier draft that misread the deferrals as merit judgements.

## Recommendation

Treat the deferrals as capacity allocation, not as a verdict on the work. Rabbie's dev capacity is
the binding constraint and the board prioritised Deposits. Nothing was deferred because it was
judged weak.

That changes what is worth arguing. Do not relitigate any of the five on merit; that was never the
conversation being had. Where a deferred need persists, propose the cheapest thing that fits inside
the capacity that remains, and accept that some of these wait until capacity changes.

The one exception worth pressing is IA restructure, which is deferred on funding rather than
capacity and is a different kind of decision.

## Why

| Deferred | To | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| My New Account | Q3 mid-year review | Dev capacity. Deposits prioritised by the Rabbie's board |
| Loyalty recognition | Q3 mid-year review | As above |
| IA restructure | Next budget conversation | Funding, not capacity. Needs its own line |
| Plan multiple tours in one trip | Q4 | Capacity, plus limited value against a declining US market |
| Basic wishlist and account | Q4 | Capacity. Cheaper interim options exist |

Deposits taking priority is coherent with the rest of the account. It is the enabling piece for
selling multi-day earlier, it answers the objection that asking for a full holiday payment nine
months out is a lot, and it gives early marketing something to say. If scarce dev capacity goes to
one thing, that is a defensible one.

The consequence still needs stating. Behaviour the deferred work was meant to serve keeps appearing
in research: manual comparison with spreadsheets and tabs, one participant texting links because
there was no way to share, another keeping a trip budget by hand. Those needs stay unserved. That is
a cost of the capacity constraint, not an error in the decision.

IA restructure is the separable case. It scores 0.900 on RICE, higher than the navigation work it
sits under, and it is the dependency for the design system. Deferring it on funding compounds cost
in a way the capacity deferrals do not, because every template built without an agreed structure has
to be reworked later.

## What this rests on

- A10: the current frontend can carry the backlog without a rebuild. This matters more now. If dev
  capacity is the binding constraint, then what each initiative costs against the current frontend
  is the thing determining what gets built at all.
- A11: the backlog adds up to the 1% target. Deferring this much work is only safe if the target is
  reachable without it, and nobody has modelled that.
- A12: UK growth is the better bet than US recovery. Two deferrals carry a US-decline rationale
  alongside the capacity one.

## What would change this

Dev capacity increasing, through hiring, a rebuild that lowers per-initiative cost, or work moving
to an external team. That is the variable, not priority.

Deposits landing and failing, which would make the capacity spent on it the wrong call and reopen
the question of what should have gone first.

## Options considered and rejected

**Arguing the deferrals on RICE score.** Rejected. RICE ranks value against effort; it says nothing
about whether the effort is available. Bringing a score to a capacity conversation misreads the
conversation.

**Proposing the cheap substitutes as a replacement for My New Account.** Not rejected, but framed
wrongly before. Notify-me sits inside the pre-released tours initiative at 4 days, still to do.
Email-me-this-tour is not on the tracker at all. Both are small enough to fit in capacity Deposits
does not use, which is the argument for them. They are not a case against the deferral.

**Escalating IA restructure now.** Rejected on timing. It goes to the next budget conversation, and
the case is stronger with effort scoping from the frontend question attached.

## Next

Put notify-me and email-me-this-tour forward as small items sized to fit alongside Deposits rather
than instead of anything. Neither currently has an owner.
