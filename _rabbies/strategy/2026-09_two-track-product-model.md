# Day tours and multi-day tours are separate funnels

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Decision effectively taken across deliverables from around June 2026; never written down as a decision.

## Recommendation

Treat day tours and multi-day tours as two products with two funnels in every deliverable, roadmap item and measurement, and refuse combined-funnel analysis as a matter of course. Where a single figure has to be quoted, split it or say which product it describes.

## Why

They are structurally different purchases. Multi-day tours are trip anchors, booked before flights and accommodation. Day tours are itinerary fillers, booked once the trip already exists. That difference determines marketing timing, page content weight, and where conversion is lost.

The Edinburgh basket data makes the point on its own. 13 in 100 multi-day viewers reach the basket against 14 in 100 for day tours, so interest is near identical. Of those, 13 in 100 pay on multi-day against 38 in 100 on day tours. Same level of interest, three times the last-step loss. That is doubt about a large purchase, not a marketing problem, and it belongs to the website work.

The clearest evidence for the decision is negative. The time-from-finding-us-to-booking chart, split by day and multi-day viewers, produces two identical lines. That is an artefact of the method: people are sorted by pages viewed, not by what they bought, so anyone who looked at both is counted in both groups. Day tours are around 80 in 100 sales, so most bookings in the multi-day group are day tour bookings anyway. Two identical lines are what that method returns whether the products behave alike or not. It proves nothing in either direction and should not be quoted again.

## What this rests on

- A1: the four personas describe the market rather than just people who bought. The two-track model is drawn from the same booker-weighted sample, so a segment the personas miss could behave differently again.

Beyond that, this rests on directly observed structural difference rather than on an untested belief. It is the most secure position in the engagement.

## What would change this

Sales tagged by product showing the two funnels converge once the counting artefact is removed. That is the test worth asking for, and it is the reason to push for product-tagged sales data rather than page-view segmentation.

A materially different basket picture outside Edinburgh would narrow the claim without breaking it.

## Options considered and rejected

**One funnel with product as a segment.** Rejected. It is how the misleading chart got produced, and it makes the multi-day last-step loss invisible inside a day-tour-dominated average.

**Three tracks, splitting European from UK multi-day.** Rejected for now. Plausible, but no evidence separates them and European tour research has not been run.
