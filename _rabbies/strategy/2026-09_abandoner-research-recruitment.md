# Getting Segment B recruited

**Date.** 07/09/2026
**For.** Yourself.
**Status.** Draft. Supersedes an earlier draft that wrongly framed this as an uncommissioned gap.

## Recommendation

Treat this as a method problem, not a commissioning problem. Segment B is scoped and on the research
roadmap; it has not been recruited because moderated video calls are the wrong instrument for people
who have already discounted the brand. Field the Flock Hub AI-moderated pilot against abandonment
signals rather than continuing to recruit for calls.

Two things follow. Run the pilot on Segment B specifically, not on an easier segment where it will
succeed for reasons that do not transfer. And treat the pilot itself as the deliverable worth
selling, since it produces a reusable capability rather than one study.

## Why

Three waves have been fielded and they close more of the non-booker gap than the caveats currently
suggest. Segment A, 18 bookers. Segment C, 6 competitor bookers who chose someone else and had some
knowledge of Rabbie's. Segment D, 6 active planners with no Rabbie's knowledge, recruited through
People For Research. Between C and D, the engagement has evidence from people who did not buy and
people who had never heard of the brand.

What is genuinely missing is narrower and harder: people who reached the Rabbie's site and left. It
is the only wave that observes the moment of leaving rather than reconstructing it afterwards, which
is exactly why the substitutes do not cover it.

The recruitment barrier is structural. A 45 minute moderated call asks for engagement from the one
segment defined by disengagement. No incentive fixes an ask that is disproportionate to the
respondent's interest in the subject. Catching them at the signal, in the moment, with a low-cost
conversational instrument, changes the trade the respondent is being offered rather than sweetening
it.

The cost of the gap is concrete and currently accruing: Deposits at 0.55 confidence and being
scoped, Buy Box sub-tasks at 0.5 with design under way, late availability with a persona marked
"To discover" on a page about to receive top-of-navigation traffic.

## What this rests on

- A1: the personas describe the market. The competitor and active-planner waves have improved this
  materially. What remains uncovered is the abandonment moment specifically.
- A3: the 92% drop-off is addressable rather than category-normal. Never benchmarked, and Segment B
  is the only route to testing it directly.
- A8: deposits recover hesitant bookers. Confidence low and unresolvable without this wave.

## What would change this

The pilot failing on the same segment for a different reason. Signal-triggered recruitment assumes
abandonment intent is detectable early enough to intervene, and that people will engage with an AI
moderator at a moment of low commitment. Both are untested.

If the pilot returns thin or unusable transcripts, the fallback is an exit survey, which establishes
stated reasons rather than behaviour and is a materially weaker instrument.

## Options considered and rejected

**Continuing to recruit Segment B for moderated calls.** Rejected. Same method, same barrier.

**Raising the incentive.** Rejected. It changes the price, not the ask, and it skews the sample
toward people motivated by the incentive rather than the subject.

**Recruiting abandoners through the third-party recruiter as with Segment D.** Rejected. People For
Research can reach active planners because they self-identify as in-market. Someone who abandoned
Rabbie's specifically is not a panel-addressable population, and self-reported abandoners recruited
weeks later are reconstructing, which is the thing this wave exists to avoid.

**Piloting Flock Hub on an easier segment first.** Rejected, though tempting. A pilot that succeeds
where recruitment was never the problem proves nothing about the case it was built for.

**Treating it as an out-of-scope commission to sell.** Rejected as the framing. It is on the
roadmap already. Presenting it as new scope would invite a conversation about whether it is needed
rather than about how to get it done.

## Next

Two smaller things worth pairing with it, both cheaper and both closing named gaps: two or three UK
last-minute booker interviews to de-risk late availability, and the private tours interviews, where
non-converting enquirers face the same reachability problem and would benefit from the same
instrument.
