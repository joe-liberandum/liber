# Sources

Where the raw material lives. This file is a pointer, not a store. Raw transcripts are not committed to this repo.

**Fill in `RAW_ROOT` before using.** Everything below is relative to it.

```
RAW_ROOT = <set this, e.g. ~/Sunny Lemons/Clients/Rabbies/raw>
```

**Naming convention for anything added from here on:** `YYYY-MM-DD_participant-slug_segment.ext`

**Handling rule.** Transcripts contain full participant names. Any document that leaves the repo uses initials and a segment label only. Do not copy a name out of this folder into a deliverable.

## Trusted Returner evidence, previously unrecorded

Carrie H, 22 April 2026, is a repeat Rabbie's customer interviewed while actively planning. She is the only participant in the entire evidence base who is both a returner and observed in market rather than reconstructing after the fact.

Her stated process: check whether Rabbie's runs a tour from where she is, compare on Viator, TripAdvisor and Yelp, and come back to Rabbie's nine times out of ten. She follows Rabbie's on Facebook and Instagram and named their current social video content as the strongest thing they do to pull people toward a tour.

This matters because `personas.md` recorded that no direct participant quote existed for the Trusted Returner. It also complicates the persona: she does comparison shop, she just converges. See the dissent note in `../personas.md`.

She also flags a recruitment channel not documented anywhere: a form linked from a Scotland travel group post. She noted on the call that she was unsure she fitted the screen because she had already used Rabbie's, which suggests the screener was aimed at non-bookers.
