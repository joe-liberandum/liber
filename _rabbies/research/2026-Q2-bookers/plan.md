## 2. Research briefed, not yet run

**European Tours booker research.** Brief produced. Not scheduled.

**Private Tours booker research.** Moderator guide produced for enquiry-to-booking handoff interviews. Structural gap already identified: non-converting enquirers are unreachable through booker-only recruitment. The guide needs a recruitment answer before fielding, not after.

**Segment B, site and cart abandoners.** Scoped and on the research roadmap. Not recruited. Moderated video calls are the wrong instrument for a segment defined by disengagement: a 45 minute ask is disproportionate for someone who has already discounted the brand. Planned fix is the AI-moderated interview pilot through Flock Hub, triggered on abandonment signals, which catches people in the moment rather than asking them back. This is the wave that most changes the persona confidence ratings. See `../strategy/2026-09_abandoner-research-recruitment.md`.

**UK last-minute bookers.** 2 or 3 interviews in an April to June window would materially de-risk the late availability initiatives. The original research included no late bookers. Separate commission.
