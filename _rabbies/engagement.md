# Engagement

Sunny Lemons Ltd, contracted by Liberandum Consulting Limited, working for the ultimate benefit of Rabbie's Tours.

Source of truth: `SOW - Rabbies Full UX & Research`. This file is a working summary. Where the two disagree, the SOW wins.

---

**How we got here.**

There have been two engagements, not one.

*First, 2025.* A discrete project to improve the Direct Packages booking flow, with user testing support. Signed 04 August 2025. This is what the SOW signature date refers to.

*Then, from January 2026.* The full year UX and user research support engagement described below, secured off the back of that first project.

The SOW header date of January 06 2025 is a typo for 2026. The three dates in the document are not a conflict once the two engagements are separated.

Practical consequence: Direct Packages predates the current contract. Phase 1 of that work shipped under the earlier engagement. Phase 2 sits inside the current one. When referencing Direct Packages history, be clear which engagement a decision was made under.

**Scope.**

Comprehensive user research, UX strategy, design consulting, and ongoing strategic support across the full Rabbie's customer funnel. Six project areas:

- Multi-market user research across 3 key markets
- Strategic design system development and documentation
- Ongoing CX strategic support and advisory
- Continuous customer research and voice of customer synthesis
- UX design execution support and quality assurance
- A/B test strategy and behavioural design guidance

Delivered in two parts.

*Phase 1: Growth Design Foundation.* 24 moderated user sessions, 8 per market across 3 markets. Market specific journey maps. CX funnel analysis report with a functional design lens. Acquisition trigger framework for marketing. Prioritised recommendations roadmap. Video highlights and a research repository. Alongside that: an audit of existing design patterns, a growth optimised component library, responsive templates for key pages, developer handoff documentation, design governance guidelines, and training materials for the internal team.

*Ongoing monthly support.* 60 hours per month, split three ways.

| Package | Hours per month | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| A. CX Strategic Support | 12 | Monthly strategic review with leadership. Quarterly roadmap prioritisation. A/B test strategic support (2 hrs). Behavioural design guidelines for new features. Performance analysis from a CX perspective. |
| B. Customer Research Support | 24 | Quarterly research sprints, 6 participants (12 hrs per quarter). Monthly voice of customer synthesis (4 hrs). Usability issue tracking (2 hrs). One feature validation per month (6 hrs). |
| C. UX Design Execution Support | 24 | Design system maintenance (6 hrs). Feature design reviews, 2 to 3 per month (8 hrs). Developer support and QA (4 hrs). Component documentation (6 hrs). |

Actual working shape has evolved beyond the SOW wording into a multi-initiative delivery programme. See `research/plan.md` for what is live. Anything that has drifted materially from the above needs a written change note under section 7 of the SOW.

**What success means.**

The contractual measure is a 1% or better improvement in conversion rate, achieved through evidence based design decisions, multi-market user insight, and systematic customer experience optimisation.

Underneath that headline, the working measures are:

- Reduced abandonment across the interest and desire phases, which is where roughly 92% of drop off currently sits.
- First time visitors reaching the right tour without needing prior brand knowledge.
- Qualification friction moved upstream, out of the booking flow and onto the PDP.
- The four personas adopted as the shared customer frame across all three agencies: CRO backlog at Liberandum, SEO content at Salience, product initiatives at Sunny Lemons.
- Decisions traceable to evidence, with evidence strength stated.

Acceptance criteria per the SOW: research reports on client approval after presentation and feedback; design system on delivery of library, documentation, and completed training; monthly deliverables on delivery of documented outputs and completion of allocated hours; strategic reviews on delivery of recommendations and supporting documentation.

**Out of scope.**

Explicitly excluded by the SOW:

- User research beyond the 3 specified markets
- Software development or code implementation
- Copywriting services
- Marketing campaign execution
- Third party tool procurement or licensing

Available as chargeable additional services: user research £80/hr, usability testing £80/hr, UI design £75/hr, content strategy £80/hr, copywriting £45/hr. Additional market research beyond 3 markets is quoted on request.

Practical boundaries worth holding in day to day work:

- Non-booker, cart abandoner, and competitor-only research is not covered by the original research commission. It is the single biggest evidence gap. Treat it as a separate commission, not as absorbed scope.
- UK last-minute booker interviews sit in the same category.
- Product catalogue decisions, for example introducing 3 to 4 day tour durations, are Rabbie's decisions. Sunny Lemons can evidence the gap but does not own the call.
- Any frontend rebuild is a Rabbie's engineering decision. Sunny Lemons can scope initiative effort against current versus rebuilt frontend as input to it.
