# Mobile PDP module order above the fold

**Initiative.** Mobile PDP layout optimisation. Priority 12, **RICE rank 1**.
**Type.** Test before build.
**Status.** Not scheduled. 4 days effort, the cheapest high-reach item on the backlog.

---

**Hypothesis.** Mobile paid social traffic is not converting because decision-driving content (reviews, group size, route map) sits below the fold, not because the traffic is poorly qualified. Reordering modules will convert traffic that already exists.

**Baseline.** Most paid social traffic is mobile. Volume is high, bookings are not.

**Variant.** Above the fold, in order: route map as lead visual, attributed reviews, small group size as a headline claim, price and availability.

**Primary metric.** Mobile PDP to booking-flow entry rate, paid social sessions only. Secondary: mobile completion to booking.

**Falsified by.** Reordering shipping with no movement. Or, cheaper and first: traffic quality analysis showing paid social sessions have materially worse intent signals than other mobile sources, for example sub-second bounce, no scroll depth, no second page.

**Run the traffic quality check first.** It costs almost nothing and it decides whether this test is worth running. If the traffic is unqualified, the highest-ranked item on the backlog returns nothing and the problem belongs to media buying rather than design. See A4 in `../../assumptions.md`.

**Decision it unblocks.** Whether mobile PDP work is a design problem or a media problem. That determination is currently assumed, not established, and it sits under the top-ranked initiative on the tracker.

**Dependency note.** The reviews module is only worth promoting above the fold if it is trustworthy. Two participants lost confidence at the current unattributed feed. Promoting a trust-breaking module up the page could make this worse. Consider sequencing after owned reviews, or testing with the reviews module excluded.
