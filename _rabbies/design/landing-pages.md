## Overall templates

**Effort TBC, RICE pending.** Reach 5, impact 3, confidence 0.75.

Likely the largest single piece of work in this group. The tracker notes that scoring the group without it would overstate the group's priority, which is correct and worth preserving.

### Problem

Landing pages are trying to do two different jobs for two different customer types on the same page, and there is no repeatable story structure guiding new pages, so every campaign is built from scratch.

One type of customer lands on a tour page ready to buy. Another returns to the homepage repeatedly over weeks. The same page serves both badly. Cited as a major factor in the 92% interest and desire drop-off.

### What is known about what works

Best-performing existing marketing pages are product-first and focused. Over-complicated pages are a documented pattern to avoid, and Rabbie's has a tendency toward them. See `patterns.md`.

### Scope

Set out story structures to guide the team and define the CRO tests to run against them. This is a template and governance piece, not a page build.

### Related, unresolved

Paid search runs campaigns for Glasgow, Killarney and Dublin. New departure pages exist but there is no clear plan for what each page type should do. Without one, every campaign stays custom built. Partly depends on the tour stop data check.

## Late availability

**In Design.** 6 days. RICE rank 10.

### The timing problem

Late availability is being promoted to the top of the main navigation. The page it links to has not been reviewed for how late bookers decide.

### What late bookers need

Fast, price-led and date-led. Clear price, date and availability without navigation. Sorted soonest first. The page needs its own setup rather than inheriting the standard PLP.

### The gap that caps this

**Persona marked "To discover."** No late-availability booker has ever been interviewed. Reach is scored 2 out of 5 for that reason, not because the segment is small. The original research included no late bookers at all.

Two or three interviews in an April to June window would materially de-risk this. Separate commission, not covered by the original research scope. See `research/plan.md`.

Designing this page without that research means designing for an imagined user on the surface that is about to receive top-of-navigation traffic.

## Private tours

**Scoping. Effort TBC. Confidence 0.6.** This is research (interviews), not build. The original 3 to 5 day estimate for private tour landing content does not apply.

### The revenue miss

One participant with a 13-guest group dismissed Rabbie's for a private booking because Facebook groups suggested day tours only. A documented, direct revenue miss.

Another described what would have won her: explicit wording that Rabbie's handles large family celebrations. She would have gone there first from a Google search. She also accepted an earlier deposit from the competitor she chose, because the reason was clear: two vehicles were being tied up.

### Private tours is a need state, not a fifth persona

From the customer insight work. The private-tour pull is a motivation layered on the existing four personas, not a behaviour cluster of its own. The people drawn to it diverge on discovery, decision pattern and value exchange, sharing only the private-tour interest.

- Primary home: Meticulous Planner, autonomy and intimacy seekers, currently going to Airbnb Experiences
- Secondary thread: Purposeful Adventurer, the large-family or special-event group

### Page workstream, now

- Bespoke testimonials from real private-tour customers. Trustpilot is the wrong proof here
- Party-size and pricing clarity for solo and small parties. The "2 to 16" line confuses
- Filterable inspiration gallery by party size and destination
- Fix the empty trust widget

### Evidence caveat

Only 2 actual private-tour buyers have been interviewed. The signals are latent demand and page defects, not a researched buyer journey. Run targeted research with private-tour enquirers and buyers before deciding whether a distinct persona is warranted.

A structural recruitment problem exists: non-converting enquirers are unreachable through booker-only recruitment. The moderator guide needs a recruitment answer before fielding, not after.
