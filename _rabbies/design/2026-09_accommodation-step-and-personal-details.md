# Accommodation step and personal details form

**Initiative.** Direct packages 2.0. Priority 2, RICE rank 11.
**Type.** Test before build.
**Status.** Overdue. The initiative is in build with a delivery date of 30 Sep 2026.

---

**The problem this test exists to solve.** Two 60% drop points in the multi-day booking flow, at the accommodation step and at personal details. The current diagnosis of the accommodation drop is that the step is doing qualification work belonging on the PDP. The personal details drop has no diagnosis at all.

**Hypothesis A, accommodation.** The drop is driven by customers arriving to gather information rather than to book, so surfacing accommodation type, price range and process on the PDP will reduce entries and reduce the drop within the step.

**Hypothesis B, personal details.** Not formed. This is the point of the test. Candidate causes: form burden, price anticipation, a natural pause to consult a partner.

**What the evidence actually supports.** Three participants entered checkout specifically to find accommodation information. That establishes the behaviour exists. It does not establish that the 60% are those people. One participant said she would back out at payment rather than at details, and described reaching the details step several times before booking, which is a decision-pause pattern rather than form friction.

**Primary metric.** Step completion rate at each of the two steps, multi-day only. Day tour flow is not comparable because it has no accommodation step in front of the form.

**Falsified by.** Testing showing the accommodation drop is price-driven or reflects genuine disqualification. Or field-level data showing the personal details drop concentrates on one field, which would make it a form problem after all and point at a much cheaper fix than the current scope.

**Decision it unblocks.** Whether Direct Packages 2.0 spends its remaining effort on the accommodation component and copy, or on the form. Those are different pieces of work.

**Note on timing.** The tracker requires user testing before design to confirm root cause. That sequencing is correct and has been overtaken. Record what was and was not tested before build, so the post-launch read is honest about what it can attribute.
