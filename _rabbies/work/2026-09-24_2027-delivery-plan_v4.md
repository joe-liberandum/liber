## The plan, up front

Rabbie's has asked for the biggest impact, cost-effectively and with minimal complexity, for the rest of 2026 and into 2027. The plan answers with three moves, in this order.

1. **Sell the second tour before they travel.** Cross-sell on the confirmation page and email (QW-15) is the most cost-effective action in the plan. A pre-arrival email sequence (SP-08) follows it by December, before the January multi-day peak. Both sell seats on departures already running, which is the 2026 focus exactly. The one thing that could stop the email half is the consent position for marketing to booked customers. Confirming it (DC-16) is an October task.
2. **Start the product data foundation now and finish it by March 2027.** It scores lower on cost-effectiveness than anything else near the top, because it is large. It goes first anyway, for two reasons the score cannot see. Tech capacity reduces in 2027, and the listing page rebuild cannot happen without it. It is a Tech build, but it is not Tech-only in its inputs. It needs the attribute definition from UX by 31/10/2026 (SP-02) and Ecommerce's answer on how products are maintained (QW-04). Without those, Tech builds a pipeline for today's data structure, which is the one that is failing.
3. **Fix listing pages in two steps.** Navigation now delivers people to listing pages that do not move them on. First, cheap fixes within current data by December: listing order and tour card copy (SP-06). Then the rebuild on the new data by June 2027: filters, cards and templates by tour type (LI-12). Its design runs in Q4 so that the build is ready the moment the data lands.

Alongside these, the highest-value small projects are accommodation content on multi-day tour pages (SP-04), the paid split by product and market (SP-10), and merchandising under-filled departures (SP-24). All three are cost-effective and land in 2026.

**Tech-heavy work that would land in the second half of 2027 is now a 2028 candidate, unless Rabbie's confirms it has the capacity.** That covers the multi-tour basket, the account and compare build, and the landing page templates. The confirmation-page and pre-arrival cross-sell captures most of the basket's 2027 value without a build.

**The single piece of information that most affects the sequence:** Tech capacity now and through 2027, and the date it reduces (DC-17). The plan assumes current capacity holds to 31/03/2027.

## How work is scored

Two numbers per action. **Impact** answers "biggest impact". **Priority** answers "cost-effectively and with minimal complexity".

| Factor | Scale | What earns the top score |
|---|---|---|
| **Seats and revenue** | 0 to 3 | Sells more seats on departures already running, at a measured loss point or to a customer already won |
| **Margin** | 0 to 3 | Removes a measured cost at scale, or sells without acquisition cost |
| **Focus fit** | 0 to 2 | 2 for secured inventory (2026), multi-day or Scotland (2027). 1 for target markets or non-Scotland. 0 for neither |
| **Evidence** | Multiplier | Strong or audited 1.0. Directional or stated 0.75. Workshop only, single voice or untested 0.5 |
| **Lands** | Multiplier | In 2026, so it earns the rest of this year and the January peak, 1.0. Q1 2027, 0.8. Q2 2027, 0.5. Second half of 2027, 0.25 |
| **Effort** | Points | XS 1, S 2, M 3, L 5, XL 8 |
| **Complexity** | Points | 0 for one team with no dependency. 1 for two teams or one dependency. 2 for three or more teams, or several dependencies |

**Impact = (seats and revenue + margin + focus fit) × evidence × lands.** The highest possible is 8.

**Priority = impact ÷ (effort + complexity).** The higher the number, the more impact per unit of cost and complexity.

These are judgements of potential, not forecasts. Enablers are not scored. They are listed with what they unlock. Decisions are gates. The manage-booking security fix (QW-01) is mandatory.

## Sequencing rules

Priority orders the work within a team's capacity. Five rules override it, in this order.

1. **Mandatory first.** QW-01, the manage-booking security fix.
2. **Gates before what they gate.** The October decisions unlock five of the top seven actions: DC-06 (accommodation scenarios) for SP-04 and LI-02, DC-16 (consent) for the email half of QW-15 and all of SP-08, and DC-02 (load factor) for SP-24.
3. **Tech work lands before Tech capacity reduces.** Any action that needs Rabbie's developers is sequenced to finish by 31/03/2027 where it can, and by 30/06/2027 at the latest. This is why the product data foundation (LI-01) starts in October, despite a priority of 0.56. It is also why the listing page rebuild (LI-12) is designed in Q4, so that its build can start as soon as the data lands.
4. **Dependencies set the floor.** LI-12 cannot start its build until LI-01 delivers the data. LI-01 cannot be built well until SP-02 defines what the data must hold.
5. **2026 volume before year end.** Where two actions tie, the one that sells secured inventory before 31/12/2026 goes first.
