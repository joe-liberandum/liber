# What our customer data tells us

Overseas markets skew higher-value and book much further out — Canada leads on ABV (£310) and Australia on lead time (85 days) — while lead times also vary sharply by departure hub, from ~28 days to over 150.

**ABV, LEAD TIME & CUSTOMERS**

| | ABV | Lead | Custs |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK | £181 | 38.8d | 60,031 |
| US | £226 | 67.9d | 63,025 |
| Canada | £310 | 77.8d | 14,306 |
| Australia | £271 | 85.2d | 10,578 |

**US VS UK: SESSIONS, BOOKINGS & CVR (YTD)**

| | US | UK |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions (YTD) | 962,233 | 869,116 |
| Bookings (YTD) | 17,849 | 6,786 |
| CVR | 1.86% | 0.78% |

US delivers 42.6% of Direct bookings at 1.86% CVR vs UK's 16.2% at 0.78% — quality, not volume, is the lever.

Other markets make up the remaining ~41% of Direct mix — led by Canada (8.9%) and Australia (3.9%).

**● TIMING OF SECOND BOOKINGS**

37–41% of second bookings happen the same day as the first (a further 13–15% within a week). Genuine returns cluster around 1, 11 and 23–24 months later — an annual/biennial pattern, not a random gap.
