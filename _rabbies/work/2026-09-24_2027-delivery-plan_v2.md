## The plan, up front

Rabbie's makes more money in 2027 by selling more seats on departures it already runs, starting with multi-day in Scotland. Most of the cost of a departure is fixed, so an extra passenger on a running tour is close to pure margin. That is management's own statement of the business. The plan has three levers: more seats sold, lower cost to sell, and lower cost to serve. It also has one guardrail: no volume growth where the margin is unknown.

The work is 72 actions in four buckets:

- **22 quick wins.** Small, no dependencies, able to start now. Most land before the January multi-day booking peak.
- **24 small projects.** Bounded pieces of work with one deliverable, usually waiting on one named thing.
- **11 larger initiatives.** Multi-team, multi-phase builds. Eight carry forward existing tracker initiatives from the 2026 plan. Two are new parents: the product data foundation, which absorbs initiative 5, and the multi-tour basket. One is Salience's market search programme.
- **15 decisions.** No build. Ten sit with Rabbie's leadership, and none has an owner today. Naming those owners is the first action in the plan (DC-01).

Profit cannot be shown yet. Margin, load factor and commission rates are not held anywhere in the project. Until DC-02 is answered, the plan is judged on seats sold and cost to sell, which are proxies.

**The single piece of information that decides whether this plan can be judged on profit:** contribution per departure at current load factor, split by product (DC-02).

**The Sunny Lemons contract measure is out of line with these goals.** It is a 1% or better improvement in conversion rate, and the SOW does not say whether that means relative or absolute. A site-wide rate is dominated by day tours and can rise while multi-day falls. The renewal should replace it with multi-day conversion and seats sold.

## The three levers and the guardrail

| Lever | Why it moves profit | Main actions |
|---|---|---|
| **More seats sold on running departures** | Fixed cost per departure, per management. Extra passengers are close to pure margin | SP-04 accommodation content, QW-15 cross-sell, SP-18 late availability, QW-20 register interest, SP-24 merchandising under-filled departures, LI-06 multi-tour basket |
| **Lower cost to sell** | Paid spend is £706k against a £605k target, with ROAS and cost per booking behind plan | QW-07 re-baseline, SP-10 paid split, SP-11 early-window test, QW-12 mobile tour page, SP-19 owned reviews |
| **Lower cost to serve** | Staff time spent on data entry and on reassuring customers is cost without revenue | LI-01 product data foundation, DC-06 fewer accommodation scenarios, QW-14 what happens next, SP-09 day-before SMS |
| **Guardrail: no growth where margin is unknown** | Private tours use dedicated vehicles, drivers and consultant hours, and the 2027 forward book is up 116.5% | DC-13 holds acquisition spend until the margin comparison exists. QW-18 qualification at first contact is allowed |

**Timing.** 7-day tours took 23% of their 2025 bookings in January, and March is the peak booking month overall. Anything meant to lift multi-day in 2027 has to be live by early January 2027.

**One open question could redirect the seat lever.** If popular Scotland departures already sell out at peak, more conversion on those dates adds nothing, and the work shifts to shoulder season and under-filled routes. Load factor by route and month settles it (DC-02).

## The buckets

Every action sits in exactly one bucket and has exactly one lead team.

| Bucket | Test | Typical size | How it is run |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quick win** | No dependency. One team leads. No new capability | Under a week of effort | Done inside business as usual. Reported at the monthly review. Quick wins in Phase 2 are queued behind Phase 1 capacity, not blocked |
| **Small project** | One deliverable. Either up to about three weeks, or waiting on one named dependency | 1 to 3 weeks | Tracker row. Named owner, measure and date |
| **Larger initiative** | Several teams and phases, or over three weeks | Months | Tracker parent, RICE scored, with a lead team and a role for each supporting team |
| **Decision** | No build. A commercial, operational or governance choice | Hours to weeks of organisational effort | A named owner and a date, not a ticket |

A workshop item that v4 classed as a task becomes a quick win if it has no dependency, and a small project if it waits on something. The size estimates are Sunny Lemons', not developer estimates.

## The critical path

Five chains decide whether 2027 lands. A slip at the start of any of them moves its end.

1. **Profit visibility:** DC-02 load factor, contribution and commission → DC-03 Scotland target → SP-12 scorecard → every later priority call.
2. **Multi-day before January:** DC-06 cut scenarios → SP-04 accommodation content → SP-13 test → LI-02 Direct Packages rollout.
3. **Product data:** QW-03 time log and QW-04 locale answer → SP-02 attributes → LI-01 scope → DC-08 funding → LI-01 build → filters, availability, compare.
4. **Selling the second tour:** DC-07 finance decision → QW-15 cross-sell now → LI-06 basket later.
5. **Reachable customers:** QW-05 permission cause → SP-08 post-booking sequence with offers → SP-20 post-tour email → SP-22 rebook segmentation.

## How the plan is run

- **Monthly strategic review**, as the SOW provides, reports against the 2027 scorecard (SP-12), not against activity.
- **Quick wins are reported, not managed.** Small projects and larger initiatives carry tracker rows. Decisions carry an owner and a date.
- **Every action gets a measure and a baseline before it is scoped** (DC-11). The measures in this plan are proposed and need agreeing.
- **Cheap and reversible first.** Build only when volume justifies it.
- **Any change to the plan is written down**, with the reason, in the monthly review notes.
- **Sunny Lemons renewal.** The contract ends 05/01/2027. Liberandum and Sunny Lemons agree 2027 scope by 31/10/2026, with this plan as its basis and multi-day conversion and seats sold as the success measures. UX actions after 05/01/2027 depend on it. Monthly voice of customer synthesis continues under the SOW's package B.

**Not in scope, named rather than absorbed.** Research with non-bookers and basket abandoners is the largest evidence gap on the account and needs a separate commission. Copy is excluded from the Sunny Lemons SOW, so UX provides structure and content rules, and Content & Social writes the words.
